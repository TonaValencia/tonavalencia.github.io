About

Let me introduce myself.

Hi, I’m Paula, a multimedia and learning experience designer from Cali, Colombia. I’ve been working in design since 2015, across branding, web, video, educational games, and interactive content. For almost five years I worked at Corporación Talentum, mostly on educational projects with the Government of Valle del Cauca. I started as a Unity developer and later moved into design and project coordination, working on wireframes, visual direction, documentation, and pre-production. Before and after that, I worked with startups, large companies, universities, and freelance clients, which taught me to adapt to different needs, tools, and deadlines. I also studied in Japan and did a UX research internship there, which changed how I think about interfaces and how culture shapes the way people use them. I work in Spanish and English. Whether it’s a learning platform, a campaign, or a brand that needs visual content, I’d be happy to hear about it.

Valle Mágico — An Inclusive Adventure

Company: Corporación Talentum
Role: Unity developer (team member), QA tester & user-test facilitator
Tools: Unity

One of the biggest challenges of the inclusive school is identifying and recognizing the individual learning profile of each student. Valle Mágico gives teachers a tool to identify multiple intelligences (Gardner, 2001) and learning styles (Kolb, 2007). Through its information system (S.I.F.), it generates an individual profile of each student from these two psychopedagogical approaches, so teachers can design learning experiences that strengthen students' skills, and so students develop metacognition, autonomy and ownership of their learning.

I was part of the Unity development team, performed QA testing and ran user tests.
